Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app or extension?

No. VidSave works entirely in your browser—there is no backend server or installation required.

What types of Instagram links are supported?

Any public Instagram post, Reel, or IGTV link that contains instagram.com or instagr.am can be converted into a download link.

Why am I redirected to ddinstagram.com?

ddinstagram hosts a lightweight download page for the media. VidSave simply rewrites your link so you can download the video in one tap.

Can I save private videos?

No. You must have access to the content, and the link must be viewable without logging into the account of the person who posted it.
