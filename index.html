Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app or extension?

No. Fexto works entirely in your browser—there is no backend server or installation required.

What platforms are supported?

Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Twitter are fully supported, including formats like Reels, Shorts, Stories, and carousels as long as the post is public.

Why am I redirected to other domains?

Fexto uses trusted, open-source mirrors such as ddinstagram, Piped, VX TikTok, SnapSave, and VX Twitter to expose the original video files for download.

Can I save private videos?

No. You must have access to the content, and the link must be viewable without logging into the account of the person who posted it.

What about HD quality?

The mirrors Fexto opens provide the original stream qualities. Pick the resolution that fits your device—1080p for Instagram carousels, 4K for YouTube, and more.
